**Ember Pact** — an original duel card game. You are the Ashcaller, facing Sable, Warden of Cinders, across a green baize table. Both duelists have 30 life; embers (mana) grow by one each turn up to 10. Four suits — Ember ▲, Tide ●, Thorn ◆, Stone ■ — each told apart by colour *and* shape. Creatures fight on the table, spells strike at once, and Sable plays a readable game you can counter: she saves her burst for a killing blow and sends her creatures at the weakest thing they can kill.

## Run it

```sh
cd game && python3 -m http.server 8080
# open http://localhost:8080/index.html in desktop Chrome
```

Play with mouse or keyboard: `1–5` plays a hand card, `Enter` ends your turn, `Esc` cancels a selection. Screenshot presets: `?shot=start|table|win|loss&seed=N`.
